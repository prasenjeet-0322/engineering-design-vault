# ❌ Why Leaving & Why This Company?

How you explain your exit and explain your interest in their company are critical filters for cultural fit and motivation.

---

## 🚪 1. "Why Are You Leaving?" Script
*Rule: Focus on growth and the future (pull), never complain about your current company or manager (push).*

### 📝 Strategic Template:
> *"I've had a great experience at `[Current Company]` where I got to own `[Project/System]` and learn how to `[Technical Skill]`. However, at this stage in my career, I'm looking to challenge myself with `[specific technical area, e.g., low-latency event-streaming / multi-master databases]` at a larger scale. Since my current team's roadmap is now focused primarily on maintenance, I am looking for a team where I can design systems under `[target scale challenge]`."*

*   **Your Draft**:
    *   *Srikar's Draft:* "I've loved being a Founding Engineer at Saavik Solutions, where I got to scale Kridaz and EA Overseas from zero to 7k+ DAU. It taught me absolute ownership and deep systems profiling. However, I want to apply my database routing and concurrency design skills to problems that occur at millions of transactions per second. I'm looking for a company with a larger user base and complex distributed data systems where I can continue to grow as a high-scale systems engineer."

## 🏢 2. "Why [Company]?" Script
*Rule: Do not give a generic answer. Pull details from their engineering blogs, open source contributions, or product launches.*

On my current team, I had to solve a similar problem with `[Your Project]`, and I faced limitations with `[Specific technology/pattern]`. I'm excited to bring my experience with `[Your Tech]` to a team that is solving `[Their Challenge]` at this massive scale."*
